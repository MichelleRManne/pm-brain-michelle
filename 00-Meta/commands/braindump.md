# /braindump

Start a product sense coaching session. Think before frameworks.

## What Claude does

1. **Stays in braindump mode**
   - Does NOT suggest frameworks or templates yet
   - Asks 3–5 open-ended, situation-specific questions
   - Draws from `02-Methods-and-Tools/2.0-Foundations/2.0.1-Mental-Models/6-Product-Sense-Development/2-product-sense-prompts.md`

2. **Checks context**
   - Asks whether to bring in relevant context from `01-Company-Context/`, `03-Research-Artifacts/`, or `04-Initiatives/`

3. **Probes assumptions**
   - Separates what you know from what you're guessing
   - Surfaces at least one risk or second-order effect
   - Surfaces at least one uncomfortable thought
   - Reference: `PRODUCT-SENSE-RULES.md`

4. **Only after braindump is sufficient**
   - Summarizes what it heard
   - Proposes 1–2 candidate frameworks that fit — not before

## When to use

- Starting any significant product decision
- Before writing a PRD, strategy doc, or opportunity assessment
- When you have a hunch but haven't articulated it yet
- When you're being asked to move fast and feel like you're skipping steps

## Related commands

- `/framework` — when braindump is done and you're ready to structure
- `/navigate` — when you need to find the right phase or approach first
- `/start` — when the braindump confirms this is a new initiative
