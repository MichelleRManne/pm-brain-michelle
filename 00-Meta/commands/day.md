# /day

Daily planning ritual. Structure your day before it structures you.

## What Claude does

Runs three check-ins depending on when you trigger it:

**Morning (default)**
- Fire drill check: anything urgent that changes today's plan?
- Commitments check: what did you promise someone that's due today?
- Focus list: what's the one thing that must get done?
- Protect at least one 2-hour deep work block
- Reference: `02-Methods-and-Tools/2.3-Execution/2.3.1-Daily-Execution-And-Rituals/1-daily-rituals.md`

**Midday check**
- Progress vs. plan: on track or off?
- Fire drills that landed: anything to re-prioritize?
- Afternoon protection: what do you still need to protect?

**Evening wrap**
- Loops closed: what got done?
- Loops open: what carries to tomorrow?
- Tomorrow prep: what do you need to set up tonight?

## Output

- Today's must-do list
- Protected focus block (time + topic)
- Fire check status
- Meeting list with prep needed
- Evening wrap items

## When to use

- Morning startup when you want an interactive planning conversation
- When your day has gone sideways and you need to reset
- When you're not sure what to prioritize

## Cowork note

If you set up a scheduled Monday briefing (see `Implementation/cowork-setup-guide.md`), it covers similar ground automatically: tracker status, notes from last week, and a parking lot nudge. Use /day for interactive daily planning and let the scheduled task handle the Monday context dump.

## Related commands

- `/focus` — if priorities are unclear after morning check
- `/week` — for end-of-week wrap or Monday planning
- `/unstuck` — if something is blocking your day
