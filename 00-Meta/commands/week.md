# /week

Weekly review and planning ritual. Three touchpoints: Monday, Wednesday, Friday.

## What Claude does

**Monday — Launch**
- Last week retrospective: what shipped, what slipped, what you learned
- This week priorities: primary goal + 3 deliverables
- Calendar gardening: what meetings need to exist, what should be cut
- Team sync prep: what does your team need from you this week?
- Reference: `02-Methods-and-Tools/2.3-Execution/2.3.1-Daily-Execution-And-Rituals/2-weekly-cadence.md`

**Wednesday — Pulse**
- Progress check: where are you vs. Monday's plan?
- Course corrections: what needs to change?
- Team pulse: anything surfacing that needs your attention?

**Friday — Wrap**
- Shipped: what actually got done?
- Learnings: one thing that surprised you this week
- Carried over: what moves to next week and why?
- Stakeholder update: draft your Friday update (ready to paste)
- PM Brain feedback block: how did the repo and AI workflow serve you this week? (used / frustrated / missing / one small action)

## Output

- Week theme (one line)
- Monday plan: primary goal, deliverables, focus blocks, watch items
- Wednesday check-in: progress, course corrections
- Friday wrap: shipped, learnings, carried over, stakeholder update draft

## When to use

- Monday morning for interactive week planning
- Friday for a structured retrospective conversation
- Mid-week when something has shifted and you need to re-plan

## Cowork note

If you set up the scheduled tasks in `Implementation/cowork-setup-guide.md`, several overlap here:
- **Monday Morning Briefing** — runs Monday morning, pulls tracker status and notes, no interaction needed
- **Friday KPI Pulse** — runs Friday afternoon, pulls metrics, drafts a stakeholder narrative
- **Sprint Retro Prep** — runs the night before retro, reads the sprint's tickets

Use **/week** when you want a thinking conversation about the week. Let the scheduled tasks handle the data pulls and first drafts.

## Related commands

- `/day` — for daily execution within the week
- `/focus` — for priority conflicts mid-week
- `/unstuck` — if something isn't moving
