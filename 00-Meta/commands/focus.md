# /focus

Cut through noise. Identify what actually matters right now.

## What Claude does

1. **Captures your current load**
   - Asks you to list everything competing for your attention (brain dump of obligations, not structured)

2. **Runs Eisenhower sort**
   - Urgent + important → do now
   - Important, not urgent → schedule and protect
   - Urgent, not important → delegate or timebox
   - Neither → drop or defer
   - Reference: `02-Methods-and-Tools/2.0-Foundations/2.0.1-Mental-Models/4-Execution-Hygiene/2-eisenhower-matrix.md`

3. **Applies LNO framework**
   - Leverage (high output per hour) vs. Neutral vs. Overhead
   - Reference: `02-Methods-and-Tools/2.0-Foundations/2.0.1-Mental-Models/4-Execution-Hygiene/3-lno-framework.md`

4. **Handles context switching**
   - If juggling multiple initiatives: defines context zones
   - Reference: `02-Methods-and-Tools/2.3-Execution/2.3.1-Daily-Execution-And-Rituals/3-context-switching.md`

5. **Outputs a clear prioritization**
   - What to do now (this session or today)
   - What to schedule (specific time)
   - What to delegate or decline
   - What to drop entirely

## Output

- Quadrant sort of current obligations
- Protected deep work blocks with topics
- Boundaries to set (what to say no to)
- What to decline or defer with suggested language

## When to use

- When too many things feel urgent at once
- Before your weekly planning when priorities feel murky
- When you've been context-switching too much and lost your thread
- When someone asks you to add something and you're not sure if you should

## Related commands

- `/day` — once focused, plan today with protected blocks
- `/week` — for weekly time allocation
- `/unstuck` — if focus issues are a symptom of something deeper
- `/navigate` — if unclear which initiative to prioritize
