Guide a product leadership transition for: $ARGUMENTS

Read and chain these skills in sequence:
1. `02-Methods-and-Tools/pm-skills/skills/altitude-horizon-framework/SKILL.md` — Anchor leadership model
2. `02-Methods-and-Tools/pm-skills/skills/director-readiness-advisor/SKILL.md` — Diagnose current readiness
3. `02-Methods-and-Tools/pm-skills/skills/vp-cpo-readiness-advisor/SKILL.md` — For executive transitions
4. `02-Methods-and-Tools/pm-skills/skills/executive-onboarding-playbook/SKILL.md` — Build execution plan

Checkpoints:
- Identify where transition friction is actually occurring (scope, horizon, systems, narrative)
- Clarify decision rights and expectations with stakeholders
- Define evidence-based milestones for first 30–60–90 days

Re-run quarterly for recalibration. Pair with /strategy if you also need to reset product direction.
