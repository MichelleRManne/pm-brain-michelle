# Decisions Observed

Decisions made by **others** (in Slack, meetings, async threads) that affect your scope. Capture them as they happen. Chat history expires or gets buried, so the source disappears fast and this file becomes the institutional memory.

Append with **"log observed decision"** in any Claude session, or run `/log-observed` for a structured capture.

This is **separate** from `decisions-log.md` (which is for *your own* decisions, with full rationale and alternatives you weighed). Different purpose, different trust level on the rationale.

---

## Format

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Date observed** | YYYY-MM-DD — when you noticed/heard the decision |
| **Decider** | Person or group who made the call |
| **Decision** | One sentence — what was decided |
| **Rationale** | Why this was chosen — prefix `[INFERRED]` if you're guessing |
| **Impact on my scope** | Why this matters to what you own |
| **Source** | Slack channel, meeting name, or thread title (no permalinks — they may expire) |
| **Open questions** | What you'd want to verify with the decider if you could |
| **Confidence** | `firsthand` (you were in the room) · `secondhand` (heard from someone who was) · `inferred` (pieced together from signals) |
