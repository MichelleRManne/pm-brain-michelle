# Jobs To Be Done — AI Toolkit

These are the core jobs this system needs to do. Use these to evaluate outputs and push back if something doesn't serve them.

## JTBDs

1. **Data Synthesis & Insights**
   Ongoing synthesis of customer, utilization, and financial data into patterns and hypotheses — not just summaries.

2. **Structured Thinking**
   Frameworks and templates for strategy and roadmap development. Braindump first, then structure — never the reverse.

3. **Requirements & Change Management**
   Generate requirements at speed. When something changes, identify every place of impact and update systematically — this was the BMAD capability I most want to preserve.

4. **Task Partners**
   Analysis support and brainstorming with specific personas on demand (data analyst, exec reviewer, UX, eng perspectives). One persona at a time, no dev loop.

5. **Prototyping**
   Visual ideas I can share with teammates quickly — mockups, flows, rough UI — without needing engineering bandwidth.

6. **KPI Tracking & Analysis**
   Define core metrics, run analyses, build projections. The system should know my KPIs and apply them without me re-explaining each session.

7. **Exec Artifact Generation**
   Strategy and roadmap docs formatted for leadership. Concise over comprehensive by default.

## What I loved about BMAD and want to preserve

- Structured movement between strategy and execution
- Change impact identification across all affected areas
- Multi-discipline expertise at my fingertips
- Workflow guardrails so I don't miss steps
- Systematic parking lot that captures ideas without derailing the current task
